Kipoharu does not include Sentry, session replay, automatic diagnostic uploads, machine tracking identifiers, or remote performance telemetry.

## Local diagnostics

Application errors and logs may be stored locally on your device. They are not automatically sent to the upstream Koharu developer or to Kipoharu's maintainer. The application does not upload screenshots for diagnostics.

## Network features

Features you choose to use, such as model downloads, online fonts, translation providers, or other connected services, may still contact their respective services. Those connections are separate from diagnostic telemetry.

## Questions

Please use the Kipoharu repository's issue tracker for questions. Do not post passwords, unpublished pages, credentials, or other private files in public issues.
